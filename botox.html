Dissolving Migrated
Filler with Hylenex

Patient Presentation

Patient presented with a small lump several months after receiving dermal filler. She had already tried oral steroids and antihistamines without improvement before seeking a consultation with Dr. Choudhary.

Dr. Choudhary's Approach

Following thorough examination, the site was identified as migrated filler. A precise spot injection of Hylenex — an FDA-approved hyaluronidase enzyme — was administered directly into the affected area. The bump resolved completely.

What is Hylenex?
Hylenex (hyaluronidase) safely dissolves hyaluronic acid-based fillers such as Juvederm and Restylane — used when filler has migrated, over-corrected or caused an adverse reaction. Works rapidly, often within hours.

Common Questions

Botox FAQ

How long does Botox last? +
Botox typically lasts 3–4 months. With consistent treatments, many patients find results lasting longer over time as the muscles learn to relax.
Does Botox hurt? +
Most patients describe Botox as a tiny pinch. The needles used are very fine and the treatment takes just a few minutes. Topical numbing cream is available upon request.
When will I see results? +
You'll begin to see results within 3–5 days, with full effects visible at 10–14 days. We offer a complimentary 2-week follow-up to ensure you're happy with your results.
Is there any downtime? +
There is no downtime with Botox. You can return to your normal activities immediately. We recommend avoiding strenuous exercise and lying flat for 4 hours post-treatment.
Who is a good candidate for Botox? +
Most healthy adults who want to reduce the appearance of dynamic wrinkles are good candidates. A consultation with Dr. Choudhary will determine if Botox is right for you.
